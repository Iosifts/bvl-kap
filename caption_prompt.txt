Describe this artwork for a blind or visually limited reader.

Write a single continuous description covering these aspects in order:
1. The main scene or subject — what the artwork shows overall.
2. The spatial arrangement of the most important visible elements — where key objects appear (foreground, background, left, right, center), what figures are doing, their poses.
3. Visual qualities that aid understanding — light, color, texture, material, only when relevant.

Rules:
- Be precise, factual, and concise.
- Do not invent details that are not visible.
- If something is uncertain, say so briefly (e.g., "appears to be," "suggests").
- Prefer accessible, natural wording over art-historical jargon.
- Focus on what is useful for non-visual understanding.
- Do not include symbolic, historical, or psychological interpretation unless clearly marked as such.

Write plain prose only. No bullet points, no numbered lists, no markdown, no bold text, no layer labels.
